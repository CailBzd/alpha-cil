## architecture

Fair — the RLS/SECURITY DEFINER data-security boundary is genuinely solid, but the `packages/*` module boundary described in `aidd_docs/INSTALL.md` has drifted heavily: business logic and CRUD live almost entirely in `apps/web` instead, and the artisan persona duplicates auth-guard/layout code that the other two personas already share.

Boundary that *does* hold well: `packages/intervention` (ademe/sirene) and `packages/notifications` (mailer) are only ever imported from Route Handlers (`apps/web/app/api/**/route.ts`), never from pages/components — that narrow adapter boundary is respected. The RLS/`SECURITY DEFINER` design (e.g. `supabase/migrations/0011_acces_carnet.sql`, `0030_rendez_vous.sql`) is also sound: ownership checks are non-recursive, `SECURITY DEFINER` functions are reserved for cases RLS genuinely can't express (anon token-based consultation links, cross-persona lookups into `auth.users`), and `middleware.ts:21-23` correctly does session-refresh only with no ACL logic, matching the INSTALL.md decision.
