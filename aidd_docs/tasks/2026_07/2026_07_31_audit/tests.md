## tests

Poor — automated test coverage is confirmed at zero (no `*.test.ts`, `*.spec.ts`, `__tests__/`, `playwright.config.*`, `vitest.config.*`, `jest.config.*`, or CI workflow anywhere outside `node_modules`); every one of the 14 shipped stories plus 14 extra features has been verified only by manual click-through and Playwright screenshots per commit message, so the "pyramid" isn't inverted, it's simply absent at every level.
